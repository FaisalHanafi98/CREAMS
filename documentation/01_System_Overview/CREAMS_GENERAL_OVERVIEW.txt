SYSTEM OVERVIEW
============================================================================

CREAMS is a comprehensive Laravel 10.x-based management system designed 
specifically for rehabilitation centers in Malaysia. The system manages 
trainees, staff, activities, assets, and administrative operations across 
multiple rehabilitation centers.

TARGET USERS:
- Administrators (System-wide management)
- Supervisors (Centre-level management)  
- Teachers (Activity and trainee management)
- AJK (Committee members with limited access)
- Trainees (Self-service portal)
- Parents/Guardians (Trainee progress monitoring)
